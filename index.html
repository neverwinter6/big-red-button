The premise

A small protest against the infinite scroll.

The internet is very good at showing you more of the same thing. This is the opposite: one button, one tangent, one article at a time.

Every press asks our small backend for a random article from Wikipedia's featured and good article collections. That means the writing has been reviewed, the facts are sourced, and the subject is almost certainly something you weren't looking for.

No tracking. No accounts. No recommendation engine. Your browsing memory stays in your browser, only so you don't get the same article twice.
